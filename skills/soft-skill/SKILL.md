# Soft Skill

## Core Principles
- **Soft Shadows**: Use large blur radii and low opacity for depth.
- **Ample Whitespace**: Don't crowd the content. Let it breathe.
- **Spring Motion**: Animations should feel like physical objects with weight and soft bounce.
- **Pastel & Muted Tones**: Avoid high-saturation colors.

## Aesthetic Guide
- Font: `Outfit` or `Inter`.
- Borders: Rounded corners (16px+).
- Backgrounds: Suble off-whites or deep charcoal with blue/purple undertones.
